Important Verification Footnotes

Original Document Retention

Original Transfer Certificate (TC) and Migration Certificate will be permanently retained after eligibility verification. Keep 5–10 photocopies for your records.

Photocopy Submission

Bring 2 complete sets of crisp, self-attested photocopies of all marksheets alongside the original certificates.

Foreign & NRI Students

AIU Equivalence Certificate, valid Student Visa, and UGC/AICTE compliance certificates are mandatory for international curricula.

Name Mismatch Guidelines

Any discrepancy in candidate name across marksheets and Aadhaar must be supported by an official Government Gazette notification or affidavit.
